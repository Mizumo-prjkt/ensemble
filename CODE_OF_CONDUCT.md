# Contributor & Community Code of Conduct

## Our Purpose & Vision

Ensemble was created with a clear and passionate mission:
- **Lessening Barriers to Writing Fanfiction:** This software was designed from the ground up to lower the barriers for authors writing, formatting, and organizing fanfiction and creative stories. By integrating rich text editing, live AO3 Work Skin styling, chapter management, lore mapping, and seamless export in one distraction-free application, we aim to empower fan authors of all experience levels to tell their stories freely.
- **Open Source Contribution under MIT:** Ensemble is developed and released as free, open-source software under the permissive **MIT License** as our way of contributing back to the global open-source and creative writing communities.

---

## Core Disclaimers & Principles

We invite everyone to use, study, and contribute to Ensemble in an environment rooted in mutual respect, creative freedom, and digital safety. By participating in this project (as a user, contributor, or plugin author), you agree to adhere to the following standards:

### 1. Zero Tolerance for Discrimination and Illegal Conduct
- **No Discriminatory Behavior:** Discriminatory remarks, hate speech, bigotry, racism, sexism, homophobia, transphobia, xenophobia, or harassment targeting any individual or group will not be tolerated under any circumstances.
- **No Illegal Use or Contributions:** The software must not be used for illegal activities. No discriminatory, malicious, pirated, or illegal code, assets, or commits will ever be accepted into this repository.

### 2. AI-Assisted Development Notice & Transparency
- **Transparent AI Development:** This project's source code, documentation, and tools are actively developed and maintained with the assistance of Artificial Intelligence (AI). 
- **Freedom of Choice:** We believe in full transparency. If you personally dislike, do not support, or prefer not to use software developed with AI assistance, **we are completely okay with that**. You are under no obligation to use Ensemble; excellent human-crafted alternatives exist (such as Obsidian, Google Docs, LibreOffice, Microsoft Word, or Scrivener). We simply ask that you refrain from trolling, brigading, or harassing maintainers and users regarding the use of AI tools.

### 3. Anti-Harassment & Welcoming Community
- **Harassment-Free Environment:** Harassment of any kind—including personal attacks, trolling, derogatory comments, stalking, or publishing private information without consent—is strictly prohibited across all project channels (GitHub issues, pull requests, discussions, and external forums).
- **Constructive Communication:** Critique ideas and code constructively, not the people behind them.

### 4. Adherence to Security Policy Guidelines
- All contributors, issue reporters, and users must follow the project's **[Security Policy](SECURITY.md)**.
- Report security vulnerabilities, exploit vectors, or sandbox bypasses privately and responsibly through the channels designated in `SECURITY.md`.
- Do not publish zero-day exploits, proof-of-concept attacks intended to harm users, or publicly expose vulnerabilities before patches are released.

### 5. Plugin Authoring & User Property Protection
- **Do Not Harm Users' Property:** Community plugin authors must hold the highest standard of care for users' machines, files, and data.
- **Strict Prohibition on Malicious Code:** You must never develop, publish, or distribute `.er` plugins that:
  - Attempt to damage, alter, encrypt, or delete user files outside the designated private storage.
  - Attempt to bypass memory caps, instruction limits, or thread boundaries.
  - Exfiltrate manuscripts, personal credentials, or browsing history.
  - Redivert network traffic to unauthorized third-party servers, phishing links, or unvetted hosts.
- Any plugin attempting rediversion or security boundary violations will be immediately and permanently quarantined by Ensemble's sandboxing engine and reported to the developers.

---

## Enforcement & Reporting

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards of acceptable behavior and will take appropriate, fair corrective action in response to any behavior that they deem inappropriate, threatening, or harmful.

Maintainers have the right and responsibility to remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, pull requests, issues, and other contributions that do not align with this Code of Conduct.

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported by opening a confidential inquiry or contacting the maintainers via the repository's issue tracker.
